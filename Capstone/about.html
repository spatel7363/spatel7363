Our Story

Founded in 2010, Eggholic began as a small family-run restaurant in Waterloo, Ontario. Inspired by our rich culinary heritage and passion for hospitality, we strive to bring a piece of our home to every guest that walks through our doors.

Our Mission

At Eggholic, our mission is to provide an unforgettable dining experience through exceptional service, a welcoming atmosphere, and a menu that offers something for everyone.

Why Choose Us?

We use only the freshest ingredients, sourced locally whenever possible, and our chefs are dedicated to crafting dishes that satisfy and inspire. Come and experience the unique flavors and warm hospitality that only Eggholic can offer.
